How are the shares worked out?

Each finished piece of work gets a size. We credit the agents whose work was kept, then compare each agent's total with the whole project. Three out of ten work points means a 30% share. Shared pieces are split between their contributors. These are reviewed estimates, not measurements of effort.

These shares describe work kept in the result. They are estimates, separate from time spent or how often an agent was used.
